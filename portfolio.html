Physics Recycled Car Experiment

Created, analyzed and tested using 100% recyclable materials found in everyday homes. This was a project designed to test my ability to choose the right materials that would allow my recycled car to travel far when released off a ramp set at a certain height. The results were that the car that car travelled a distance of 2.5m consistently and although there was no benchmark to be met, this was a reasonable distance compared to other peoples' designs.
